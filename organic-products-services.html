Frequently Asked Questions

Is all of your produce certified organic?

Yes. Everything we grow is certified organic and inspected every year. Products from partner farms carry their own organic certification.

Where do you deliver organic food boxes?

We deliver within 50 km of the farm every Tuesday and Friday. Enter your postcode on the Contact Us page to check your area.

Can I pause or skip a delivery?

Yes. You can skip, pause or cancel your organic box subscription at any time up to 48 hours before delivery.
